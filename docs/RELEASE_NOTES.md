## Nasc 1.0.0

First release.

- Password and SSH-key login, trust-on-first-use host keys
- Saved hosts, multiple tabs, background sessions
- Generate or import keys, encrypted with the Android Keystore; optional app lock
- Command autocomplete from your own history

**Install:** download `nasc-1.0.0.apk`, open it on your phone and allow installs from your browser/files app when asked. Verify the download with `SHA256SUMS`. Requires Android 8.0+.
